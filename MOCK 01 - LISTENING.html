Part 1 • Questions 1–8 (8 Marks)

For questions 1–8, choose the best answer (A, B or C). You will hear each piece twice.

1You hear a film review on the radio.
What criticism does the reviewer make?
2You hear two students talking about a holiday abroad.
What do they agree about?
3You hear a woman talking on the radio about a place called Dolphin Bay.
Where did she first hear about Dolphin Bay?
4You overhear a woman talking outside the post office.
Why had she gone to the post office?
5You hear part of a discussion on local radio.
What is the programme about?
6You hear a man talking about motorcycling.
What does he regret?
7You overhear a student talking about her new college.
How does she feel about the college?
8You overhear a driving instructor talking to a learner after the lesson has ended.
What mistake did the learner make?
